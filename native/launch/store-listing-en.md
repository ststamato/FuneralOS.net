**Description** (4000 max)
```
FuneralOS is the complete operations platform for US funeral homes — from first call to closed case.

CASE MANAGEMENT
Log every case from first call through removal, preparation, viewing and service. Full timeline, notes, priority flags and assigned director per case.

DOCUMENT TRACKING
Death Certificate, Burial Permit, Cremation Authorization, Contract, Invoice — tracked per case with Missing / Pending / Complete status. Add your own document types to match your state or county's requirements.

STAFF & CERTIFICATIONS
Assign directors and removal techs to each case. Automatic alerts when a license or certification is expiring within 30 days.

FLEET MANAGEMENT
Track your hearse, removal van and limousine — availability, mileage, next service due and insurance expiry.

CREMATION WORKFLOW
Step-by-step cremation authorization tracking, crematory scheduling and urn selection, tied to the case record.

FINANCE DASHBOARD
Case value, outstanding balance and payment status per case. See total revenue and unpaid balances at a glance.

SERVICE SCHEDULING
Assign viewing rooms, service dates, staff and vehicles from a single scheduling board — every case, every slot, no conflicts.

SMART OPS DIRECTOR
A live ops score, smart alerts and a daily briefing generated from your active cases.

CLOUD SYNC + OFFLINE
Your data syncs instantly across devices. A smart offline queue retries automatically when signal returns.

TEAM COLLABORATION
Invite directors, assistants and staff. Role-based access (Admin / Editor) keeps everyone in sync. Up to 5 members on Pro, unlimited on Business.

PLANS
• Free — 5 cases/month, no credit card required
• Pro — unlimited cases, staff, fleet, finance dashboard
• Business — everything in Pro, plus AI Smart Ops Director and unlimited team members

Cancel anytime. Upgrade or downgrade whenever you need to.
```
