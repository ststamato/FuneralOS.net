Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

FuneralOS (funeralos.net) is an independent software product. For any privacy-related matter, contact us at funeralos.net@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email & password: Used to create and manage your account, stored securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Application data: Cases, staff, fleet, finance and other funeral home records you enter are stored securely on our servers (Supabase / AWS), accessible only to you and your office's team members. The app also keeps a local copy on your device so it keeps working offline, which syncs automatically once you're back online.
  • Case documents: Files you upload per case (e.g. death certificates, permits) are stored in a private storage bucket (Supabase Storage), accessible only to your office.
  • Device notification data: If you install the iOS or Android app, we store a device identifier so we can deliver push notifications to you.
  • Payment data: On the web app, transactions are processed by Lemon Squeezy. On the iOS/Android apps, subscriptions are processed through Apple's/Google's in-app purchase systems, with subscription management handled via RevenueCat. We never store your card details ourselves.
  • Support requests: If you submit a support request, your message may be automatically analyzed by AI (Anthropic Claude) to help us process it faster.
  • Technical logs: Error logs may include IP address and browser agent for debugging purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the service.
  • To verify active subscription status.
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset, billing notices).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data Storage

All of your office's data (cases, staff, fleet, finance, settings, documents) is stored securely on our servers, via Supabase (AWS infrastructure). Database-level access policies (Row Level Security) restrict access to you and your office's team members only — never to other users of the service. The app also keeps a local copy on your device for offline use, which syncs automatically with the server once you're back online.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app (Settings → Delete Account), or request deletion from us.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Portability: Export your data at any time using the built-in export feature.

To exercise any right, contact us at funeralos.net@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

We only use strictly necessary cookies to maintain your session (authentication token). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Authentication and database infrastructure.
  • Lemon Squeezy (lemonsqueezy.com) — Payment processor for the web app.
  • Apple / Google & RevenueCat — Process subscriptions on the iOS/Android apps through Apple's/Google's in-app purchase systems, with RevenueCat handling subscription management.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (firebase.google.com) — Delivers push notifications on the iOS/Android apps.
  • Resend (resend.com) — Sends account-related email (verification, password reset, team invites).
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — Automatically analyzes support requests you submit, to help us process them faster.
  • xAI (x.ai) — Powers the AI assistant. When you use the AI assistant, the relevant case details (e.g. names, location, notes) are sent to xAI to generate the response, subject to xAI's own privacy policy.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Upon deletion request, we will remove your authentication data within 30 days. Browser-local data is under your control and deleted whenever you clear your browser storage.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. For significant changes, we will notify you via email at least 14 days before they take effect.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests: funeralos.net@gmail.com
